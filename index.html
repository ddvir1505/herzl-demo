Live demo: indoor transmitter localization with Sionna ray tracing and a neural network

A research project by Dvir Dahan, supervised by Prof. Haim Permuter

What the demo shows

A closed concrete room of 10 x 8 x 3 m with a pole in the middle. One base station with an array of 8 antennas is mounted on a wall. You draw the path of a phone inside the room, at varying heights, and the system finds where the phone is, using only the signal received at the base station.

Every point of the path (one every 5 cm) is ray traced live with Sionna: the signal propagates, reflects off the walls, floor, ceiling and pole, and reaches the antennas. A trained neural network then estimates the position of the phone from the received signal. The phone height is known to the network; it estimates the position on the floor plane. On 30,000 test positions never seen in training, the mean error is 5.6 cm.

At the end you see the true path and the estimated one in two rooms side by side, raw and smoothed, with the overlap between them.

How to use it

Best on a computer with a mouse (not on a phone).

  1. Choose a mode with the buttons at the top:
    • Half circle (two points): click a start point and confirm (Confirm), then an end point and confirm. A half circle is drawn between them, with the height rising and falling along it.
    • Free drawing: hold the left mouse button and draw. Releasing the button ends the path.
    • Single point + ray tracing: click one point and confirm. The rays from that point are drawn in the room, on their way to the base station (brighter and thicker = stronger), together with the position estimated by the network and the error for that point.
  2. Change the drawing height with the mouse wheel (also while drawing), the arrow keys ↑ ↓, or the Drawing height slider. The transparent blue plane shows the current height; the chair in the corner helps to judge heights (seat at 45 cm).
  3. Rotate the room by dragging with the right mouse button, and zoom by dragging with the middle button.
  4. Click Confirm and localize. The estimated path is drawn point by point in two rooms: raw and smoothed. The slider above the rooms sets what counts as overlap: 1 to 5 cm between the paths.
  5. To start again: Redraw.

One visitor at a time. After 10 minutes without activity, or a minute with the page in the background, the demo is freed for the next visitor.
